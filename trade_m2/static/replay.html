Replay settings

Use the CSV from Alerts history. Connect Upstox before running.

LONG buys the nearest CE; SHORT buys the nearest PE. The stock entry price in the CSV fixes the nearest strike; if absent, the last completed stock candle is used. Entry is the next one-minute option candle open. Target and stop use option premium. Whole lots must fit the per-trade budget. Exits use the first target or stop touch, or the final minute ending at 15:20 IST. There is no brokerage or historical bid/ask in this study.

One-minute OHLC cannot prove the order of two threshold touches within a minute or guarantee an exchange fill. Ambiguous candles assume the stop first. Missing data and Upstox Plus restrictions are reported explicitly. Today's completed candles and contract lists are saved in the local database for later review.
