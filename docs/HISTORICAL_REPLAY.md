# Historical alert replay

Open `/replay` after connecting Upstox, or use the link in the paper terminal. Export the chosen date from **Alerts history**, upload that CSV, choose an option expiry and enter the option-premium target, stop, and per-trade budget. The result table and downloadable CSV include every input row, including skipped rows and provider errors. Replay results are separate from the live paper account and never submit orders.

The CSV must contain `tradingsymbol`, `trade_side` (`LONG` or `SHORT`), and timezone-aware ISO `event_time`. The Trade M3 export also contains `entry_price`, which is used as the underlying spot at the alert. Without it, replay uses the last completed one-minute stock candle. One upload accepts up to 500 rows and 2 MB.

For each row, the module finds the exact NSE equity symbol and contracts for the selected expiry. LONG chooses CE, SHORT chooses PE. It chooses the closest listed strike to the stock price, with lower strike winning a tie. It does not silently choose a cheaper distant strike. The entry is the first available option one-minute candle open strictly after the alert, before 15:20 IST. One lot costs that opening premium times the contract lot size; if one lot exceeds the budget, the row is `OVER_BUDGET`. Otherwise it buys the largest whole-lot quantity the budget permits. Results have no fees or slippage.

The first subsequent candle touching the option-premium target or stop decides the hypothetical exit. When both are touched within the same minute, the module assumes the stop first and flags uncertainty. When a later candle opens beyond a threshold, its open is used. If neither is touched, a full session exits at the 15:20 IST boundary using the 15:19 candle close. Missing candles before the boundary produce `INCOMPLETE_HISTORY`, without invented P&L. Other rows can report `NO_CONTRACT`, `NO_ENTRY_CANDLE`, `PLUS_REQUIRED`, or another precise data status. OHLC does not show historical bid/ask or intraminute order, so these are scenarios rather than verified fills.

Contract lists and completed historical candles are cached in the local SQLite database under `data`. Preserve that folder when upgrading. Today's candles refresh during the day; after they become a past date, a saved exact request remains available offline. For uncached expired contracts, Upstox's expired-instrument APIs may require Upstox Plus. A login and entitlement are required to fetch uncached data. For a same-day expiry, run the replay while the expiry is still current to save its contract list and candles locally.
