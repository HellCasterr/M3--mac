# Trade M3: option strike research and forward paper model

## Direction and research

A LONG stock alert maps to buying a call; a SHORT alert maps to buying a put. Selling a put would be bullish, so it is not the SHORT implementation. The strike is the exercise price, not a success rate. The [Options Industry Council delta guide](https://www.optionseducation.org/advancedconcepts/delta) explains directional sensitivity, while its [theta guide](https://www.optionseducation.org/advancedconcepts/theta) and [pricing guide](https://www.optionseducation.org/optionsoverview/options-pricing) explain why time and volatility alter premiums. No delta or strike promises a 10% premium target. [Upstox option contracts](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/get-option-contracts/) and [option chain](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/get-pc-option-chain/) supply actual strikes, expiries, lots and current quotes.

For the nearest listed expiry passing the editable minimum-days filter (default zero), search the nearest ATM and two near ITM strikes for a CE or PE. Prefer correctly signed delta with absolute value 0.45–0.75, small spread, positive volume/open interest and displayed depth; rank by closeness to 0.60 delta, spread and volume. When that screen fails, use the nearest **quoted and affordable** ATM/near ITM contract as an explicitly marked fallback. If those cost more than the budget, consider the three nearest quoted OTM strikes and label the affordable choice as a budget fallback. Never invent a quote, contract, lot or historical fill. Some stocks have no options or no affordable contract. Wider spreads can cause an immediate negative bid mark even if the stock moves favorably.

## Forward simulation

A new eligible alert after auto mode was enabled is enriched with an option candidate and schedules a virtual entry at the first option trade reported at or after the next one-minute boundary. This matches replay's next-candle-open rule (a 09:34 alert enters at 09:35). The option-chain LTP at selection time is only a sizing estimate; the actual next-minute price must fit the per-trade budget and available virtual cash. If no option print arrives during the next minute, the entry is cancelled. No broker order is sent. Positions are marked for exit by 15:20 IST, so late entries can have a shorter hold. A manual ticket can override budget, lots, target, stop and maximum hold. The default +10% target and −5% stop apply to the **option entry premium**. A duplicate alert cannot create a second trade. Market hours and the expiry filter still apply. Auto mode is initially off and persisted settings apply to future trades. Starting cash cannot be changed after the first trade.

The first subsequent valid live bid crossing either level closes all remaining lots at the observed bid in the same quote update, with no artificial latency, slippage or fee. Manual, time and session exits use a valid bid when available; a gap can exceed the nominal stop loss. If an exit remains unfilled through 15:30, across the next session boundary, or past contract expiry, the trade moves to `CLOSED_UNCERTAIN`. The record retains its unresolved quantity, marks its exit order `UNCERTAIN`, and creates no synthetic fill. It leaves option subscriptions, and account P&L totals become unavailable because its exit value is unknown. A stale or out-of-order quote cannot create a fill. Existing trades keep their settings. Previous alerts are not replayed, and no backtest is performed. Older recovered signals whose market event was over four minutes old when detected are explicitly historical. The stock dashboard orders alerts by generated time and labels recovered candle time separately.

SQLite persists decisions, trades, orders, fills and sampled option quotes. The terminal exposes virtual cash, realised and unrealised P&L, open positions, order history, fills and CSV export. No broker order endpoint is called. The first reported option trade at or after the next minute boundary supplies the virtual entry, and subsequent feed bids supply exit marks and fills. The live tick is an approximation of the one-minute candle open; feed gaps can cause an entry to be cancelled, and bid exits may differ because of depth, spread, impact and missing ticks. No result from this model should be treated as real-money net profit.

## Verification

Automated tests cover immediate entry, first observed target/stop, gap-through-stop, budget and cash constraints, duplicate entry, old settings migration, fallback selection, stale quote rejection and live alert auto submission. Live authenticated Upstox market-hour verification remains necessary on the user's machine. Review missing candidates, connection status, subscription health and exported fill records during the next session.
